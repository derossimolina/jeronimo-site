**Jerônimo de Rossi Molina** is a political analyst and a PhD candidate in Administration at the University of Caxias do Sul. He writes, checks and approves the final copy of everything the brand publishes.

> Fact and document come first. The position comes after, in the headline, short and supported.

## Analysis

The **jeronimo molina** brand publishes political and geopolitical analysis with fact, source and position, in a newsletter, a podcast and videos. The position appears in the headline, and the fact that supports it comes with document, date and source.

Besides published analysis, the brand provides political, geopolitical and economic risk analysis consulting to companies, with the same method and the same open sources.

## Research and PhD

The PhD informs the method. It is not the seal of the piece: what we publish stands on documents and data, not on academic authority.

## How we report

1. **Fact with source.** Every relevant piece of information points to a document, official data or a traceable source.
2. **No document, no claim.** What has no document goes in as a question or stays out. We do not publish what rests only on an anonymous source without independent confirmation.
3. **Position in plain sight.** The position appears in the headline and in the text, and the fact that supports it has a source.
4. **Data before adjectives.** Whenever possible, numbers, series and public documents.
5. **Fact, inference and scenario kept apart.** Readers know what is confirmed, what is interpretation and what is projection, with a probability range and confidence level when it is a scenario.
6. **Fast correction.** Factual errors are corrected within 24 hours and flagged to readers.

Sources and credits are recorded in a transparency file, with documents and data in view.

## Where we write from

The lens is **liberal-progressive**: a smaller and predictable State, open markets, individual liberties and equality before the law. It is not neutral and does not pretend to be. It is not militant either: we do not cheer, we do not campaign and we do not take the agenda of either pole.

We write from the standpoint of those who produce, invest, hire and answer for decisions under uncertain rules. That is why our agenda asks what changes in cost, deadline, tax, contract, predictability and regulatory risk.

- **Economy:** less State, a credible fiscal rule, tax simplification, trade openness, privatizations and concessions, legal certainty for contracts and freedom to do business.
- **Individual liberties:** freedom of expression, a secular State, autonomy over one's own life, equality before the law.
- **Against privileges:** super-salaries, party and electoral funds, non-transparent budget amendments, unevaluated tax breaks, corporatism of any kind.
- **Institutions:** defense of the rule of law and of representative democracy, with transparency and fiscal responsibility. A decision by a court, agency or oversight body is criticized on its text and merit; the institution is not delegitimized.

## How we cover the federal government

Criticism of the federal government is a coverage rule, not an exception. Every relevant decision on spending, taxes, regulation, state-owned companies, public credit, foreign policy and relations with Congress is measured against the government's own discourse and against the outcome.

Criticism always has four parts: **the act, the date, the rule or the number, and the effect**. We do not attribute intent, we do not use backstage theories and we do not use adjectives in place of data.

**The same yardstick applies to everyone.** The opposition, the other branches and state and municipal governments are judged by what they do and propose, with the same criteria. Criticizing the government is not an endorsement of those who oppose it, and getting one point right does not absolve the rest.

Rumors, chain messages, fraud claims without proof and attribution of hidden motives do not enter as a source or as a rhetorical question.

## Tone

Combative against acts and ideas, never against people. Criticism is aimed at decisions, proposals and results. No nicknames and no personal attacks.

The voice has three requirements, in this order: **direct** (the conclusion is in the first line), **dry** (short sentences, no cheering adjectives; when we do not know, we write "we do not know") and **credible** (those who disagree check the document and find no factual error).

## Consulting and editorial separation

Besides published analysis, the brand provides political, geopolitical and economic risk analysis consulting to companies. We do not name clients. Both activities use the same method and the same open sources, and these rules apply:

1. **The agenda is not the client's.** Clients do not choose, review or veto editorial pieces, and do not receive favorable coverage.
2. **Conclusions are not commissioned.** The conclusion of an analysis does not change because of who pays, and no deliverable contradicts what we publish about the same fact.
3. **Client material does not become a piece.** Client information, requests and identity are confidential.
4. **Ties are declared.** When a piece deals with a topic involving an active client, the tie is stated in the piece itself.

The brand does not lobby, does not broker access to public officials, does not run election campaigns, does not issue legal opinions and does not recommend investments.

## Who pays

The brand is funded by **subscribers and advertisers**. If there is another source of revenue, it will be listed on this page.

- **Identified sponsorship.** Every sponsored insertion is marked as advertising and kept apart from editorial content.
- **Free agenda.** Sponsors do not choose, review or veto editorial content, nor receive favorable treatment in coverage.
- **Conflict of interest.** We do not accept sponsorship from anyone who is the subject of ongoing critical coverage without declaring the tie to readers.

## Professional associations

Participation of the editor in professional associations is declared here and, when the association or one of its members is the subject of a piece, in the piece itself. Participation does not condition coverage: the association is criticized for what it decides, with act, date, rule or number.

Status on 23/11/2026: no participation.

## Artificial intelligence and images

We use artificial intelligence tools to organize information. Final writing, fact-checking and approval are human, by the editor.

Photos are always real and credited. We never use AI-generated images.

## Corrections and contact

Factual errors are corrected within 24 hours, with notice to readers. To request a correction or reach the newsroom, reply to any edition of the "Jeronimo Molina" newsletter (jeronimomolina.substack.com).

## Party membership

The editor has been **a member of the Podemos party since May 2026**.

We say so here and in the piece itself when it deals with the party or its direct opponents. Membership does not ground the analysis: the position comes from the lens and the document, not from the party label. The party may change sides; the editorial line does not change with it.

If a piece's conclusion coincides with the party's position, we check the coincidence against the source before publishing. We do not declare support for candidates or parties in our pieces.
